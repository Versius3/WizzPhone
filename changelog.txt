• L1 Password reset tool link: when a password reset or an account unlock is finished in the L1 tool on ADM06/ADM07, WizzPhone fills in the user name and ticks the matching category by itself — “AD unlock account”, “AD pw reset” or both, and NSK, AIMS or eCrew pw reset.
